# Maintenance picture (severe service)

**Law:** the manufacturer's schedule in the owner's manual (or the severe-service
chart in the same book) is the authority. This page is a reading aid so a customer
knows what to *ask*, not a substitute for that book.

Central Florida driving (heat, short trips, stop-and-go) is **severe service** for
almost every maker. If the book has two columns, use the severe one.

## Typical buckets (not a quote)

These are common US passenger-car *patterns*. Intervals vary by make, model, oil
type, and turbo vs. not. Never invent a mileage for a specific car.

| Bucket | Often includes | Ask the shop |
|--------|----------------|--------------|
| Oil / filter | Engine oil and filter | "Severe-service interval for this VIN?" |
| Rotate | Tire rotation, pressure, tread | "When did they last rotate?" |
| Cabin / engine air | Filters | "Are they restricted?" |
| Brake fluid | Hygroscopic — heat and age | "When was it last flushed?" |
| Coolant | Mix and interval are OEM-specific | "Is this the right coolant family?" |
| Transmission / differential | Fluid service on many units | "Does this VIN have a service interval or 'lifetime' with a severe exception?" |
| Spark plugs / fuel | Tune items | "What's the OEM interval on this engine?" |
| 30 / 60 / 90 / 120k | Deeper inspection + the items above that come due | "Which of these are due *now* on this odometer?" |

RepairTech lists factory scheduled maintenance, oil and filter, tune-ups, fluid
services, and 30/60/90/120k services on [autorepair-tech.net/services](https://autorepair-tech.net/services.html).
That is the shop menu, not a price list.

## How to use this in chat

1. Ask year / make / model / miles / (engine if they know it).
2. Tell them to check the glovebox book or the shop can pull the OEM schedule.
3. Point at the severe-service column.
4. Safety items (brakes, steering, fuel smell, overheating) jump the queue — see
   `urgency-guide.md`.
5. No prices. Booking: https://autorepair-tech.net/schedule or (407) 348-3400.

## Hybrid / diesel / European

Same law: OEM book first. Those powertrains have extra items (high-voltage coolant,
DPF, euro inspection I/II). Do not flatten them into a generic 5k oil speech.
The shop services hybrids, diesel, and European cars — send them there for the
actual list.
