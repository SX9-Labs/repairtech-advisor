# How to read your RepairTech diagnosis report

Your report looks like this:

```
--- REPAIRTECH DIAGNOSIS REPORT ---
Vehicle: 2003 Honda Accord V6
What I noticed: rough idle
  When it started: cold start
  What it feels like: shudder
Trouble codes the scan found: P0171
Shop's leading diagnosis: System too lean, bank 1 — 75% of the shop's evidence points here
Test the shop will run to confirm: smoke test intake
Proposed work (shop job codes): FUE-001-DIA
--- END REPORT ---
```

**Line by line:**

- **What I noticed** — your concern, in your words. The diagnosis starts from what you experienced.
- **Trouble codes** — codes your car's computer stored (like P0171). A code points at a *system*,
  not a specific broken part. It's a clue, not a verdict.
- **Shop's leading diagnosis** — the most likely cause given everything found so far. The
  percentage is how strongly the evidence points at this cause. It is **not** a guarantee — that's
  why there's a confirming test.
- **Test the shop will run to confirm** — the shop confirms before it condemns a part. This test
  is what turns "most likely" into "confirmed." It's a sign of a careful shop, not an upsell.
- **Proposed work** — the shop's internal job code(s) for the repair. Prices come from the shop's
  estimate, never from this report.

**Good to know:** the report never contains your name, phone number, or full VIN — it's safe to
paste into an AI assistant.
