# Good questions to ask the shop before approving work

**About the diagnosis**
- "What test confirms this, and is that test included in what I'm paying for?"
- "What did you actually see or measure on my car?" (evidence, not vibes)
- "If the confirming test comes back different, what happens to the estimate?"

**About the money**
- "Can I get the estimate broken into parts, labor, and fees?"
- "Which lines are safety, and which can I defer to next visit?"
- "Is there a used/aftermarket/OEM parts choice on this, and what's the warranty on each?"

**About the work**
- "What's the warranty on this repair — parts and labor?"
- "Can you keep the old part to show me?" (RepairTech offers this at close-out — you should not have to ask)
- "If you find something else once you're in there, will you call before doing it?"

**Perfectly fair to say**
- "I'd like to think about the non-safety items."
- "I'm getting a second opinion on this one." (a trustworthy shop won't flinch)
- "What happens if I do nothing for six months?"
